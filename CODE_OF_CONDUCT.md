# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
We want participation to be respectful, welcoming, and free of harassment for
everyone, regardless of experience, identity, or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Ask questions when something is unclear.
- Credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing private information without permission
- Repeated disruption of discussions or reviews
- Dismissing safety concerns about an installer that uses `sudo`

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other communication related to the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the
maintainer through GitHub. For security vulnerabilities, use the process in
`SECURITY.md` instead.

Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled as privately as practical. The
maintainer may remove comments, close discussions, or block participants when
needed.
